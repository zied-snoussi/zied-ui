## Conventions

- Section indexes and labels use `font-mono text-xs uppercase tracking-[0.25em]` (see `SectionHeading`).
- Body text uses `text-muted-foreground` for secondary information.
- Arabic switches the document to `dir="rtl"`: prefer logical utilities (`ms-*`, `me-*`, `ps-*`, `pe-*`, `text-start`) over left/right ones.
