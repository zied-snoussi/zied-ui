# @zied-snoussi/ui

## 1.0.0

### Major Changes

- Initial release: the component system of zied-snoussi.vercel.app as a library.
  - `@zied-snoussi/ui`: Avatar, Badge, Button, Card, Carousel, Dialog, DropdownMenu, ScrollArea, Separator, Sheet, Tabs, `cn` and hooks.
  - `@zied-snoussi/ui/effects`: SectionHeading, NodeCluster, Trace, CircuitDefs, Particles, DomeGallery, Fade.
  - `@zied-snoussi/ui/next`: ThemeProvider, Navbar, Footer, LanguageSwitcher, ThemeToggle, SectionHeader, ScrollToTop, ProjectCard, AIChatbot and chat parts.
  - `theme.css` design tokens (light and dark) and EN/FR/AR messages.
  - Storybook 10 with docs, a11y checks, theme and locale (RTL) toolbars.
